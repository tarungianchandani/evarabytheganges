Welcome

Welcome to Evara

A Riverside Home in Rishikesh, Made for Slow Living

Some places are meant to be lived in. For us, that place is Rishikesh.

After seven years of living and working in Rishikesh, we found ourselves deeply connected to the city — to the rhythm of the Ganga, the warmth of its people, the peaceful mornings, and the mountains that make every day feel a little slower.

Evara by the Ganges was born from a simple dream: to create a home in Rishikesh that we could share with others.

Located in Nirmal Bagh, Rishikesh, Evara is a boutique riverside stay designed for guests looking for a peaceful and comfortable place to stay near the Ganges. It brings together the comfort of a home with the experience of living close to the river, while keeping you connected to the best of Rishikesh.

Here, mornings begin with the gentle presence of the Ganga. Days can be spent exploring the ghats, discovering the spiritual side of Rishikesh, enjoying good food, or simply slowing down with the people you love.

We created Evara for travellers who want more than a place to sleep — a place to pause, reconnect and feel at home.

Whether you are visiting Rishikesh for a peaceful getaway, a family holiday, a spiritual retreat, or simply a few days away from the rush of city life, Evara invites you to experience Rishikesh at your own pace.

Come for the Ganges. Stay for the feeling of home.

Our Location

Tucked away in Nirmal Bagh Visthapith, Rishikesh, Evara by the Ganges offers a quieter way to experience the city.
